# Vision

## Mission

Build a transparent, privacy-conscious, and reproducible laboratory for learning and developing medical imaging engineering practices, while presenting Felipe Angelo's progress without overstating experience or results.

## Vision

Over multiple years, FAMAI Lab aims to become a well-organized public portfolio demonstrating progressively stronger work in medical image segmentation, quantitative imaging, radiomics, medical AI, 3D engineering, digital twins, and clinical research workflows.

## Objectives

1. Document learning and technical decisions with evidence.
2. Develop reusable, tested Python workflows for public or synthetic data.
3. Publish case studies only after privacy, licensing, quality, and reproducibility reviews.
4. Create clear technical communication for international audiences.
5. Maintain an architecture that can grow without compromising simplicity.

## Principles

- **Honesty:** distinguish learning, implementation, validation, and publication.
- **Privacy:** never publish private clinical data or identifiable information.
- **Reproducibility:** document inputs, versions, parameters, and limitations.
- **Quality:** prefer small, reviewed increments over unsupported claims.
- **Simplicity:** add tools only when they solve a demonstrated need.
- **Responsible use:** never represent experimental work as clinical-grade software.

## Boundaries

FAMAI Lab is not currently a medical device, diagnostic service, clinical decision-support system, research institution, or substitute for institutional governance and regulatory review.
