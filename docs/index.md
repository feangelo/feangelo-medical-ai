# Felipe Angelo Medical AI Lab

FAMAI Lab is a developing portfolio and technical laboratory focused on learning reproducible methods for medical imaging, segmentation, quantitative imaging, radiomics, medical AI, and digital twins.

## Current scope

Phase 1 establishes governance, repository architecture, documentation, privacy controls, and lightweight quality tooling. It does not contain validated medical workflows or clinical data.

This repository is educational and experimental. Nothing here is intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment, patient management, or deployment without appropriate independent validation and regulatory review.
