## Professional direction

Practical imaging work provides context for acquisition, anatomy, image quality, artifacts, and the importance of reliable spatial information. FAMAI Lab extends that foundation through structured study and reproducible technical documentation.

The current focus is not to claim established research authority. It is to build demonstrable competence step by step: medical image geometry, segmentation quality, registration, quantitative imaging, scientific computing, and the foundations required for future radiomics and Medical AI work.

## FAMAI Lab

Felipe Angelo Medical AI Lab is a public technical portfolio and long-term digital laboratory. It separates:

- learning records from validated evidence;
- future plans from implemented work;
- professional observations from scientific conclusions;
- computational success from scientific validity;
- public, synthetic, or open data from private clinical information.

The repository is designed for work that can be inspected, repeated, challenged, and improved.

## Technical environment

Tools currently used or documented include 3D Slicer, Python, Git, GitHub Actions, MkDocs Material, and lightweight testing and formatting tools. Radiomics and Medical AI frameworks remain future learning directions until practical implementation supports stronger claims.
