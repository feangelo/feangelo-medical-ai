# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

FAMAI Lab is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment. Participants are expected to communicate professionally across differences in background, experience, identity, and perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and evidence-oriented.
- Give actionable feedback without personal attacks.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and dataset restrictions.
- Avoid medical claims that exceed the available evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disclosure of private information, publication of sensitive medical data, and knowingly misleading scientific or professional claims are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported privately through the contact method documented in the repository security policy. Public issues must not contain sensitive information. Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate these standards.

This policy is adapted in spirit from established open-source community standards and may be expanded as the community grows.
